About FitsAbroad

Most coupon sites list codes that stopped working months ago. We don't.

FitsAbroad started after one too many failed checkouts — a "20% OFF" code that turned out to be expired, or worse, never valid at all. So we built a site with one rule: every coupon gets tested by a real person before it's published, and gets re-checked on a schedule so listings don't quietly rot. We're not the biggest coupon site out there, and we're fine with that — we'd rather be the one you trust.
