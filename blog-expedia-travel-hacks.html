How to Save Money on Expedia: 5 Proven Travel Hacks (2026 Guide)

Published on September 13, 2026 • By FitsAbroad Team

Booking travel online can easily become expensive, but Expedia remains one of the best platforms to unlock hidden discounts if you know how to navigate its pricing tools. Whether you are planning a international getaway or booking a quick flight across the country, using a few strategic tricks can save you hundreds of dollars.

Here are five simple, effective ways to ensure you get the absolute lowest prices on your next Expedia booking.

1. Join Expedia Rewards Immediately

Before searching for any trip, create a free Expedia account. Expedia automatically offers instant discounts—often 10% off or more on thousands of hotels—exclusively to logged-in members. On top of direct price cuts, you earn reward points on eligible bookings that can be redeemed toward future travel stays.

2. Bundle Flights and Hotels Together

If you need both accommodation and transportation, booking them separately is usually a costly mistake. Expedia's vacation package feature bundles flights, hotels, and rental cars together at wholesale rates provided by airline and hotel partners. These bundle discounts often save significantly more than buying each component individually.

3. Use the Mobile App for Secret Deals

Expedia frequently reserves its steepest hotel discounts—sometimes up to 20% to 30% off—for mobile app users. Installing the Expedia app allows you to claim app-exclusive coupon codes and double member reward points during promotional sales events.

4. Flexible Date Searches and Price Tracking

Airfare and hotel room rates fluctuate constantly based on demand. If your travel dates aren't fixed, use Expedia's flexible date calendar to compare prices across nearby days. Tracking price trends before locking in non-refundable bookings ensures you buy when prices drop.

5. Check for Verified Coupon Codes Before Checkout

Expedia regularly issues promotional coupon codes for seasonal sales and specific destinations. Always check active deal aggregator sites before finalizing your purchase to ensure you aren't leaving extra savings on the table.
