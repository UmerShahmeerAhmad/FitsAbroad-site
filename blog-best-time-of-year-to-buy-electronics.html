Best Time of Year to Buy Electronics

Aug 11, 2026 · 5 min read

← Back to Blog

The usual advice is 'wait for Black Friday,' but tracking actual price history across a full year shows that's only true for some categories, not all of them. TVs genuinely do hit their lowest prices in the two weeks around Black Friday and Cyber Monday, sometimes 30-40% below their typical price for the rest of the year. That advice holds up. Laptops were the surprise. Back-to-school season in late July through August consistently beat Black Friday pricing on mid-range laptops in our tracking, likely because retailers compete hard for student buyers before Black Friday deals even start.
