Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FitsAbroad ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit this website (the "Site"). By using the Site, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

Information you give us directly: if you use our contact form or newsletter signup, we collect your name (contact form only) and email address, along with any message you write. This is used only to respond to you or send the newsletter you signed up for — it is never sold.

Information collected automatically: like most websites, we automatically collect some technical information when you visit — your approximate location (city/country level, from your IP address), browser type, device type, pages viewed, and how you arrived at the Site. This is collected through Google Analytics, described below.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use them, and allow trusted third parties to use them, for the following purposes:

Google Analytics: we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the Site (which pages are popular, how people navigate, roughly how many visitors we get). Google Analytics uses cookies to distinguish unique visitors. You can learn more about how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Google AdSense: we display advertising through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, or generally by visiting aboutads.info/choices if you're in the US, or youronlinechoices.eu if you're in Europe.

Third-Party Services We Use

We rely on a few outside services to run the Site, each with its own privacy policy governing how they handle data:

Formspree (contact form submissions) — formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy

Mailchimp (newsletter subscriptions) — mailchimp.com/legal/privacy

Google Analytics & Google AdSense — policies.google.com/privacy

GitHub Pages (site hosting) — GitHub's Privacy Statement

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

Respond to messages sent through the contact form; send the newsletter to people who subscribed; understand how visitors use the Site so we can improve it; and display relevant advertising to help keep the Site free to use.

Your Choices

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email we send. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some parts of the Site (like the newsletter form) may not work correctly without them. You can opt out of personalized advertising using the links listed in the Cookies section above.

Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date at the top.
